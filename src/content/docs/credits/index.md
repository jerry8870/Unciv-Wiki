## Guide illustration assets

Strategy, mechanics, first-game and achievement-troubleshooting articles use original PNGs from the game source. The wiki arranges them into teaching illustrations; they are not screenshots of a game played for these articles. The rules and conditions in the text still apply.

- Civilization, unit, building, policy, promotion and stat icons: authors and original work links are preserved in the [complete upstream credits](/game-assets/UPSTREAM-CREDITS.md), including Noun Project CC BY 3.0 / Public Domain works and separately identified sources.
- AbsoluteUnits pixel units: letstalkaboutdune, CC BY 4.0; upstream exceptions remain listed in the preserved credits.
- Fantasy Hex base terrain: CuddlyClover and contributors, CC BY 4.0. Improvement art also retains The Bucketeer / GeneralWadaling attribution under CC BY-SA 3.0. Each asset retains its original terms rather than being relicensed as one collection.
- PNG bytes are copied unchanged; the website adjusts display size and layout only. Pinned source commit: `5fa2f57457755c403ea82cc21fec9e617feaf2d0`. The [asset manifest](/game-assets/manifest.json) records each source path, SHA-256 and image dimensions.

License texts: [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

Database civilization, unit, building and technology lists and detail pages also use original icons from the same pinned commit. Per-work technology icon authors are recorded in the Technologies section of the preserved upstream credits.
