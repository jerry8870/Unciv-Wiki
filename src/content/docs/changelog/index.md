**Content baseline: 4.21.20 (1293).** These notes reflect the pinned source and rules. They do not claim every feature has been retested in a natural iOS playthrough.

- N11 and N12 accept civilization-specific equivalent buildings.
- N08, N31 and N34 use combat-experience and promotion accounting. Free, training and mixed-funded promotions do not automatically qualify.
- N08 requires the promotions before capture; N34 requires four combat promotions before its two active-attack kills in one own turn.
- N32 counts three different cities in one own turn. N35 counts four different cities, from at least two other founders, in one continuous Golden Age. Repeated cities count once within each window.
- Missing new combat fields in old saves start at zero; unknown historical experience is not reconstructed.

V3 remains 40 achievements and 600 points, with local-only records. Read the [catalogue](/achievements/) and [troubleshooting article](/guides/achievement-troubleshooting/).

Upload evidence does not establish tester availability. The [iOS page](/ios/) explains the invitation and availability boundary. This page was updated on 2026-09-12; rebuilding does not refresh that date.
