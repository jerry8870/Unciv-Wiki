This guide applies to **Gods & Kings with religion enabled**. The default victory types in this ruleset do not include a separate religious victory. Religion supports economic, cultural, scientific or military objectives. Decide what problem it should solve before committing production and faith.

## Decide whether founding is worth pursuing

Check accessible faith sources, civilization abilities and whether a Shrine would displace an urgently needed defender or Worker. Religion slots, beliefs and founding progress are competitive. If your position is weak, using available benefits from another religion can still support your main victory route.

Set a limited initial commitment: build one Shrine, observe faith income and competition, then decide whether to continue. Change priorities when an invasion threatens instead of giving every new city a religious building first. Missing your own religion is not the same as losing the game.

## Select a pantheon from tiles you will actually use

Count current and near-term workable tiles, rather than everything your borders might eventually include. A desert-related faith benefit suits land you can productively work. If it requires moving citizens onto weak food and production tiles, compare the faith gain with slower growth and construction.

Read the exact trigger for resource or connection effects. Does it apply to a tile, improvement, city or city connection? Ordinary tile yields that need a working citizen are not all collected merely because the land is inside your borders. Inspect actual city yields after choosing.

Do not rely on obtaining an entire speculative package of beliefs. Pick a benefit that can begin helping and have an alternative if your preferred later belief is taken.

## Founding and enhancement: preserve the Prophet's intended action

A [Great Prophet](/database/units/great-prophet/) can found or enhance a religion, spread it or create a Holy Site, subject to its conditions. Founding and enhancement require a Prophet that has not used other actions. Do not spread once and assume the unit remains eligible for your original plan.

At founding, inspect the remaining choices. Founder and follower beliefs benefit different recipients and have different requirements. A belief is not necessarily an unconditional empire-wide bonus. Check follower counts, majority religion, purchase eligibility and any other restrictions.

| Problem | How to select a belief | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weak finances | Inspect the gold trigger and required followers | Counting distant follower income before spreading |
| Happiness or culture shortage | Examine follower effects and faith buildings | Unlocking purchases without reserving faith to make them |
| Slow growth | Match food or growth effects to actual city conditions | Ignoring happiness and tile-assignment bottlenecks |
| Expensive spread maintenance | Evaluate pressure and spreading effects | Picking benefits unrelated to your geography |

For example, Tithe provides gold based on global followers, while Pagodas allows eligible cities following the religion to purchase Pagodas with faith. One needs followers; the other needs the right city religious state and a purchase budget. Availability depends on the current game.

## Stabilize your core before spreading outward

Make religion useful in core cities first, checking majority religion and follower counts. Pressure from nearby cities can change the balance. A single active spread does not guarantee a lasting majority. Repeatedly reconverting a peripheral city under strong competing pressure can become an endless expense.

Before buying a [Missionary](/database/units/missionary/), identify the target benefit, safe route and value of its spreads. The purchasing city's religious state affects the religion carried by the unit. Check city and unit information instead of assuming every unit you buy carries the religion you founded.

In this version, a Missionary can enter foreign territory without open borders but loses religious strength when it ends a turn there without the required access. Plan its path and target instead of leaving it wandering or waiting. Other religious units have different abilities; do not apply the Missionary's restrictions to all of them.

## Use Inquisitors and Prophets deliberately

An [Inquisitor](/database/units/inquisitor/) can remove other religions from friendly cities under its conditions and prevent active spreading to an adjacent city. It is not a permanent switch that removes all surrounding religious pressure. Continue monitoring city majorities.

Prophet spreading can remove other religions and strongly change a city's composition, at the opportunity cost of other Prophet uses. If founding or enhancement is still part of the plan, preserve an eligible Prophet instead of consuming its options on a marginal city.

## Faith eligibility is not a realized benefit

Compare the next use of faith: a core city's religious building, maintenance through spreading, Prophet-related objectives, or great-person purchases available through the appropriate policies and era. Some completed policy branches enable specific great-person purchases under conditions. A large faith stockpile does not allow arbitrary purchases at any time.

Faith, gold and production interact through buildings and population. Delaying a University for religious infrastructure can have a research cost greater than the resulting faith benefit. Keep comparing religion with the objective it is meant to support.

## Worked decision: why did choosing Pagodas not fix happiness?

Suppose you selected Pagodas when founding, but a new city's happiness did not improve. Check whether the city meets the religious conditions, whether you actually purchased its Pagoda with faith and whether the resulting effect appears in the city and empire panels.

If all faith has been spent spreading to distant cities, reserve the next budget for core purchases. If the city's majority religion changed, evaluate whether restoring it is worth the cost. This constructed example separates purchase eligibility from the completed purchase.

Reduce new religious spending when founding is no longer feasible, spread costs exceed benefits or urgent science and defense needs arise. Preserve useful existing effects and redirect resources toward [science](/strategies/science-victory/), [culture](/strategies/culture-victory/) or your other chosen victory objective.
