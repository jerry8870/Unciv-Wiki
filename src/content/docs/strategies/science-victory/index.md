Use this route for **Gods & Kings with Scientific victory enabled**. A reasonably secure practice game helps you learn the development sequence. Victory requires the Apollo Program and the required spaceship parts added in the capital. Researching a technology or finishing Apollo is not the end of the game.

## Work backward from four bottlenecks

Research unlocks parts, production builds them, aluminum and transport make delivery possible, and defense gives you time to finish. A science empire therefore still needs production cities, resources and an army capable of protecting the core.

Three or four purposeful cities are a manageable learning setup, not a victory requirement. More high-quality land can justify additional cities, but include Libraries, happiness and defense in the expansion budget.

## Early game: population, Libraries and the National College

Population contributes base science, and a [Library](/database/buildings/library/) adds science based on population. Provide food and useful tiles before expecting research buildings to carry a permanently small city.

The [National College](/database/buildings/national-college/) supplies base science and a science percentage bonus. It requires Libraries in every non-puppet city. Before expanding, check how the next city will meet that requirement. Claiming a threatened good site can still be worthwhile, but give the new city a realistic Library plan.

Place the College where science will remain strong over time, comparing population potential, existing science and production. The capital is often convenient but is not automatically the best city. Cities founded after the College is completed do not make you rebuild it.

## Midgame: turn Education into working infrastructure

Advance toward [Education](/database/technologies/education/) while obtaining necessary resource, development and defensive technologies. Complete [Universities](/database/buildings/university/) in the main science cities and employ Scientists when food permits. In this ruleset a University has a **33% science bonus and two Scientist slots**. Finishing the building does not mean its slots are staffed.

Specialists leave other work behind. If filling every slot creates starvation or removes essential production, leave some empty and improve food supply. A principal great-person city makes points and timing easier to track.

Cities directly adjacent to a mountain can consider an [Observatory](/database/buildings/observatory/) when its requirements are met. Do not force an unsafe, food-poor site into a science role solely for that building.

## Policies and Great Scientists: distinguish investment from a sprint

Rationalism opens in the Renaissance. Its opening science bonus requires a happy empire, and it is mutually exclusive with Piety in this ruleset. Adopting Piety changes the available science-policy plan; you cannot budget both branches' benefits together.

Secularism improves specialist science, Free Thought improves sources including Universities, and completing Rationalism grants free technologies. Consider when a policy begins paying back while maintaining the culture needed to reach it.

An early [Great Scientist](/database/units/great-scientist/) can create an Academy on a safe tile you will keep working. Later, accelerating research can shorten a critical technology wait. There is no universal turn at which one choice becomes better. Compare remaining game length, actual working turns, current research and the next useful unlock, then inspect the in-game action.

## Late game: research buildings and industrial scheduling

As you reach [Scientific Theory](/database/technologies/scientific-theory/) and [Plastics](/database/technologies/plastics/), add [Public Schools](/database/buildings/public-school/) and [Research Labs](/database/buildings/research-lab/) in important cities. A Lab adds percentage and specialist benefits, but a very late Lab still needs enough remaining research to justify its construction time.

Retain one or two strong production cities, prepare transport and secure aluminum. Check domestic resources, city-states and trades when supply is short. A single imported source creates expiry and war risks. Avoid discovering at the last moment that other uses have exhausted the resource needed for parts.

## After Apollo: assign all six parts

[Rocketry](/database/technologies/rocketry/) unlocks the [Apollo Program](/database/buildings/apollo-program/), which enables spaceship parts. Apollo cannot be hurried; do not rely on a Great Engineer or purchase to finish it.

| Part | Required quantity | Direct unlock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [SS Booster](/database/units/ss-booster/) | 3 | [Advanced Ballistics](/database/technologies/advanced-ballistics/) |
| [SS Cockpit](/database/units/ss-cockpit/) | 1 | [Satellites](/database/technologies/satellites/) |
| [SS Engine](/database/units/ss-engine/) | 1 | [Particle Physics](/database/technologies/particle-physics/) |
| [SS Stasis Chamber](/database/units/ss-stasis-chamber/) | 1 | [Nanotechnology](/database/technologies/nanotechnology/) |

Parts require aluminum and cannot be purchased. This table lists direct unlocks, not a research sequence that bypasses prerequisites. Follow the technology tree and begin useful available parts while researching the rest.

Build in parallel where production permits. Move completed parts to the capital, use the action that adds them to the spaceship, and verify progress in the victory screen. A finished part sitting in its production city is not yet assembled. Keep delivery tiles clear of enemy and friendly obstructions.

## Worked decision: leading in science but unable to finish

Suppose the final part technologies are within reach, but only the capital has useful production while other cities queue unrelated buildings. More science may no longer solve the main delay.

For each part, record unlock, assigned city, construction time and travel time. The last arrival identifies the bottleneck. Assign earlier Boosters to rear cities, reserve a strong producer for a late unlock and clear resources and routes in advance. If enemies threaten delivery, address them before the part appears.

At each stage, check the actual constraint: population and Libraries early, staffed Universities and happiness in the middle, production and aluminum late. Use [defensive tactics](/strategies/combat-siege/) to protect the core, and rely on the victory screen to confirm final assembly.
