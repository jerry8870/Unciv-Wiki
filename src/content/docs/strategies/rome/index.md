This guide targets **Gods & Kings, Standard speed, no mods, single-player Prince–King**. The situations below are constructed teaching exercises based on the rules, not played-game reports or verified win rates.

[Rome](/database/civilizations/rome/) accelerates buildings already completed in the capital and fields stronger early land units. Compared with [general expansion](/strategies/expansion-economy/), coordinate city queues on one timeline. For war, match objectives to Iron, roads, Ballista setup and the recovery budget.

## Ability and unique units

| Item | Effect | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Glory of Rome | +25% Production toward a building already built in the capital | The capital must have finished it; not a unit bonus or Gold purchase discount |
| Legion | 17 strength versus Swordsman's 14; requires Iron | Can build Roads and Forts, not ordinary Worker Farms or Mines |
| Ballista | 8 melee / 10 ranged versus Catapult's 7 / 8 | Retains +200% city attack, requires setup and has no defensive terrain bonus |

The ability changes construction order. It does not give free buildings or accelerate the capital's first copy through this same effect. Check the [Legion](/database/units/legion/), [Ballista](/database/units/ballista/) and [civilization entry](/database/civilizations/rome/) for requirements and prices.

## Suitable land: useful cities before city count

Satellites need worked improvements and sustainable happiness. A city with only 3 base Production remains slow with another 25%; population and a Mine may matter more. The capital needs enough production to complete shared buildings within a useful timeframe.

Assign a purpose to each settlement: food core, production, a new luxury or a strategic route. Do not queue identical buildings everywhere merely to use the bonus. A safe interior city need not copy the frontier's Walls.

## Opening branches: peaceful copying or limited war

With safe sites, let the capital lead on food, culture or science buildings that several cities need. When it must produce Settlers, Workers or emergency troops, satellites should not wait indefinitely for perfect synchronization. Build an already eligible useful project or solve their immediate shortage.

Under pressure, check whether [Iron Working](/database/technologies/iron-working/) reveals a reliable supply, whether Workers can connect it, and whether upgrades and upkeep are affordable. Without Iron, defend with existing ranged units and a front line instead of committing the whole plan to Legions. An import needs a credible duration and route through diplomatic risk.

For an offensive, name one objective before declaring: remove a border threat, obtain a resource, or capture a required original capital. Distance, poor roads and difficult siege positions can consume the unique-unit window in transit. When [Mathematics](/database/technologies/mathematics/) unlocks Ballistae, reserve production for escorts and replacements too.

## Midgame checkpoints: avoid mutual waiting

Record the capital's completion turn beside shared satellite projects. A small lead lets satellites work on food or Workers first and switch into a boosted building later. An exposed city should begin urgent defense immediately rather than wait several vulnerable turns for 25%.

[Libraries](/database/buildings/library/) are a useful coordinated project, but the [National College](/database/buildings/national-college/) still needs Libraries in all non-puppet cities. Repeated settlement can keep postponing it. Liberty fits a real land, Worker and resource plan; Tradition supports a stronger capital first. Rome has no mandatory policy tree; use [policy planning](/mechanics/policies/) for prerequisites.

Legions screen Ballistae, block cavalry and capture cities. Building Roads or Forts still costs positioning and actions; do not put the entire front line to work as the enemy approaches. Roads help reinforcement but accumulate upkeep. Secure setup tiles and escorts for Ballistae before exposing Legions to several attackers merely because a city is damaged.

## Recovery and victory

After each capture, inspect happiness, income, resources and the next counterattack. Annexation adds governance burdens; plan [Courthouses](/database/buildings/courthouse/) after resistance where annexation is appropriate. Rome's building bonus does not erase occupation penalties.

Once the objective is secured and the Legion window is fading, peace and consolidation may outperform another push with damaged troops. Repair resources, restore food and science, and shorten the front. [Domination victory](/strategies/domination-victory/) requires simultaneous control of every major civilization's original capital, including your own, not every city on the map.

Peaceful Rome can pursue [science victory](/strategies/science-victory/), using construction gains to support population and research before organizing late production. The copying ability does not directly accelerate spaceship units.

## Teaching situation one: the capital Library is finished

**Conditions:** A satellite has 8 base Production and starts a base-cost 75 Library. The capital already has the same building. Assume the standard price, no other modifiers, overflow or population changes.

**Choice:** Rome raises input to 8 × 1.25 = **10 Production per turn**. Starting from zero, rounding 75 ÷ 10 upward gives 8 turns, versus 10 turns from 75 ÷ 8 without the bonus.

**Cost:** This simplified result does not mean every city always saves two turns. Waiting a long time for the capital can lose more Science than the eventual bonus repays.

**Recheck:** Inspect the satellite's current project and production breakdown, then its actual completion estimate. If the capital is producing troops, build a needed Library independently rather than making synchronization a reason for inactivity.

## Teaching situation two: Legions cannot reach the second objective

**Conditions:** The first target is captured, income is negative, Iron is threatened, Legions are wounded and Ballistae lag behind. The next city is across a river with Forest sight blockers.

**Choice:** Stop advancing, secure and repair Iron, heal Legions and regroup the siege force. Consider peace while restoring happiness and the upkeep budget.

**Cost:** The opponent gains time and technological progress may narrow the unique-unit advantage. Continuing across the river can instead lose the army and the captured city.

**Recheck:** Read [combat and promotions](/mechanics/combat-promotions/) for river, sight and missing-resource modifiers. Resume only with sustainable supply, setup positions and postwar happiness. A Legion's 17 strength does not cancel those constraints.

## Recover a stalled empire

For slow construction, confirm the capital owns the building, then inspect population and [improvements](/mechanics/resources-improvements/). For an overloaded capital, limit its leading role to useful shared buildings and let satellites produce their own Workers or troops. For a postwar deficit, stop unnecessary road and army expansion, repair resources and restore income before another conquest.

Compare [Greece's city-state protection wars](/strategies/greece/) for a different purpose for early military strength, or return to the [guide directory](/strategies/).
