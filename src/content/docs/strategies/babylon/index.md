This guide targets **Gods & Kings, Standard speed, no mods, single-player Prince–King**. The situations below are constructed teaching exercises based on the rules, not played-game reports or verified win rates.

[Babylon](/database/civilizations/babylon/) receives an early research asset and faster Great Scientist points to extend the advantage. Compared with the [general opening](/strategies/opening/), change research and safe-tile priorities while still funding Workers, food and defense.

## Separate the two scientific benefits

| Rule or unique | Effect | Change to your play |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discover Writing | A free Great Scientist appears | Plan a safe Academy tile and a citizen to work it |
| Great Scientist rate | +50% Great Scientist point generation | Staff actual specialist slots; a percentage of zero base points is still zero |
| Bowman | 7 melee / 9 ranged strength versus Archer's 5 / 7 | A stronger defensive ranged unit still needs a screen and retreat space |
| Walls of Babylon | +6 city strength, +100 city health versus Walls' +5 / +50 | Build when city survival is the bottleneck, not automatically in every safe interior city |

See the [Bowman](/database/units/bowman/) and [Walls of Babylon](/database/buildings/walls-of-babylon/) entries for base prices. The ability is not +50% to all Science or to the immediate research awarded by expending a Scientist. The free Scientist and ongoing point bonus are separate benefits.

## Suitable terrain: how long can the Academy be worked?

A good capital needs sustainable food, defensible working tiles and enough production. Safe Grassland is a candidate, but compare displaced improvements and actual assignments. The nearest tile is not automatically best, and a high-science border tile is poor value if repeatedly pillaged or unworked.

An Academy adds 8 base Science, with another 2 each at Scientific Theory and Atomic Theory. It is a worked improvement, not an empire-wide building yield. Placement consumes the Scientist, removes removable features and replaces an ordinary improvement. Read [citizens and improvements](/mechanics/resources-improvements/) for resource-access exceptions.

## Opening branches: early Writing with a viable defense

With a safe workable site, follow Pottery toward [Writing](/database/technologies/writing/). Improve food and new luxuries alongside the research plan, then recheck growth after assigning the Academy tile. Writing's arrival turn is not the sole measure: an Academy built four turns earlier but unworked for ten turns can still lose value.

If scouting reveals an approaching army, obtain Archery, defenders and essential improvement technologies first. Bowmen improve defense efficiency but still take production. Protect the core before forcing a research sequence that loses Workers or Settlers.

Expand according to safe sites and happiness. The free Scientist does not require a one-city game, nor should expansion postpone every research building. Before the [National College](/database/buildings/national-college/), schedule Libraries in every non-puppet city. The Academy city deserves scientific investment, but compare long-term population, Science and production when choosing the College location.

## Midgame checkpoints: make the point bonus work

After [Education](/database/technologies/education/), complete [Universities](/database/buildings/university/) and assign Scientists gradually. Two Scientists generate 6 base points; with only Babylon's +50%, that becomes **9 Great Scientist points per turn**. Their base Science remains 6 before city science modifiers. Keep these accounts separate.

Favor cities with sustainable food and useful existing point progress. Do not starve every city for earlier Scientists. Tradition can support a safe Academy capital; Liberty needs a credible settlement plan. [Rationalism](/mechanics/policies/) requires the Renaissance and excludes Piety, so opening Piety for a small Faith gain changes your later science options.

For each new Scientist, compare another Academy with immediate research. An early worked Academy has time to repay its cost; a crucial defensive unlock or a final technology sprint may favor immediate research. Read the current action's award rather than following a universal turn-number cutoff.

## Finish: research must become spaceship parts

Follow [science victory](/strategies/science-victory/) through Apollo and all six parts. Prepare production cities, aluminum, delivery routes and capital security. If production or transport is the final bottleneck, additional Science alone cannot finish the parts. Reassign late cities to parts, resources and defense instead of having all of them queue more science buildings.

If war is forced on you, use superior technology to upgrade key defenders and stabilize the front. Expanding the war indefinitely is not required. A cultural or diplomatic finish needs its own established policy or city-state network; a preferred-victory label supplies neither.

## Teaching situation one: Academy or immediate research?

**Conditions:** Writing has just finished. The core is safe and a citizen can work a Grassland Academy for 30 turns. Assume the base +8 and ignore percentages and later technologies.

**Choice:** Favor the Academy: 30 × 8 = **240 base Science** over that explicit window. Also inspect the immediate research action and whether it unlocks something essential now.

**Cost:** This is gross Academy yield, excluding lost Farm food and the consumed Scientist's alternative use. Ten unworked turns reduce the window to 20 × 8 = 160.

**Recheck:** Verify assignment, food surplus and the science breakdown. If repeated pillaging is likely, protect the Scientist, stabilize the front or choose another site before placing it.

## Teaching situation two: Writing approaches alongside an army

**Conditions:** A new settlement is not established, a Worker is near the frontier, and an enemy army may reach the core in two or three turns. Libraries and an Academy are planned, but your forces cannot hold the open border.

**Choice:** Withdraw civilians, defend around cities and rivers, and produce Bowmen first. Add Walls when the city faces sustained attacks. If the Scientist already exists, protect it until a tile can be worked safely.

**Cost:** Writing, a Library or the second city may be delayed. This preserves the long-term asset; trying to recover the delay through another unsupported city can deepen the problem.

**Recheck:** Watch access to the Academy and city, screens for Bowmen and retreat tiles each turn. After pressure eases, repair food and resource improvements before resuming the Library–University sequence. Read [combat modifiers](/mechanics/combat-promotions/) before a costly river counterattack.

## Recover from a stalled lead

For an unworked Academy, restore assignments and food before adding another science building. For weak point generation, check actual base points and their city distribution. For poor growth, leave a slot empty and address food or happiness. For an unfinished spaceship despite a research lead, list the final part's production, resource and travel waits and fix the longest one.
