This guide targets **Gods & Kings, Standard speed, no mods, single-player Prince–King**. The situations below are constructed teaching exercises based on the rules, not played-game reports or verified win rates.

[Korea](/database/civilizations/korea/) gains Science from staffed specialists, worked Great Improvements and capital science construction. Compared with the [general science route](/strategies/science-victory/), manage assignments more carefully and schedule the capital's research buildings separately. Filling every slot everywhere often runs out of food first.

## Keep three science sources separate

| Source | Effect | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Specialists | +2 Science per specialist in all cities | Actual staffing, including non-Scientist specialists |
| Great Improvements | +2 Science per Great Improvement tile | An unpillaged improvement worked by a citizen |
| Capital science construction | A one-time research award for a completed science-related building or wonder | The engine checks science relevance and capital status; not every wonder or every city qualifies |
| Hwach'a | 11 melee / 26 ranged; replaces Trebuchet but lacks its ordinary +200% city-attack bonus | Useful against troops; requires setup and has no defensive terrain bonus |
| Turtle Ship | 36 strength, 4 movement; replaces Caravel but cannot enter Ocean | Strong coastal defense without cross-ocean exploration or expedition access |

A base [Scientist](/mechanics/resources-improvements/) produces 3 Science, so Korea's produces **5 base Science**. Engineers and Artists also gain 2 Science but retain their own great-person point types. Korea does not turn every specialist's points into Great Scientist points.

## Suitable terrain and opening branches

Food and production connect specialists to completed research infrastructure. Scout food tiles, fresh-water Farm potential and a defensible core. A coastal bias does not require a naval war; a coastal capital with poor food still needs improvements and food buildings.

In safety, develop food, Workers, Libraries and useful settlements into a core able to sustain University specialists. With weak food, improve Farms and grow before staffing more slots. Under military pressure, build ordinary defenders rather than waiting for unique units that are not yet unlocked.

The [National College's](/database/buildings/national-college/) Library requirement remains. Expand for useful land with a credible Library schedule. The capital bonus makes its science construction attractive, but does not justify abandoning defense to chase a science wonder.

## Midgame: Universities and the capital award

A [University](/database/buildings/university/) has a 33% Science bonus and two Scientist slots. Staff one, inspect food, growth and construction time, then decide on the second. Sustainable staffing is stronger than a repeatedly starving science city. [Secularism](/mechanics/policies/) separately adds 2 Science per specialist: a Korean Scientist with it produces 3 + 2 + 2 = **7 base Science**, before city percentages.

For qualifying capital construction, the engine awards **half the median actual cost of currently researchable technologies, rounded to the nearest integer**. It is not half the cheapest technology or a fixed free technology. Prerequisites change the researchable set, while game modifiers change actual costs. Inspect the technology tree and completion timing instead of treating base database costs as a fixed reward table.

Usually, completing a useful Library or University promptly is stronger than delaying it solely to manipulate one award. Universities outside the capital lack this particular one-time award but retain their percentage bonus, slots and contribution to a developed city.

## Unique units: defensive jobs with limits

[Hwach'a](/database/units/hwach-a/) is strong against troops but does not inherit ordinary Trebuchet city strength. Screen approach routes with melee units, check setup and sight, and clear the front line. Do not push an unescorted Hwach'a beside a city.

[Turtle Ships](/database/units/turtle-ship/) protect coastal resources, cities and local transport routes, but cannot enter Ocean. Check other units and technologies for an ocean-crossing mission; replacement status does not preserve every Caravel ability. A land war need not divert scarce production into a navy merely to use the unique ship.

## Finish and recovery

Follow [science victory](/strategies/science-victory/) through Apollo, six parts and capital assembly. Near the final research unlocks, compare moving some specialists onto productive tiles to shorten part construction. Measure the Science lost against turns saved rather than leaving every assignment permanently locked.

When food runs short, empty one or two slots and repair Farms or happiness. Under siege, protect the capital and improvements before wonders; other cities can still support population and specialist research. If Hwach'a city attacks are slow, build an appropriate siege force or stop the war rather than endlessly adding the same unit.

## Teaching situation one: staff both Scientists immediately?

**Conditions:** An eight-population city produces 22 Food and consumes 16. Two citizens work 3-Food Farms, and a University has two empty slots. There is no Secularism or other extra modifier.

**Choice:** Staffing both reduces surplus from +6 to zero and adds 2 × (3 + 2) = **10 base Science**, with 6 base Great Scientist points. Staffing one adds 5 Science and 3 points while keeping +3 food surplus.

**Cost:** Full staffing stops growth in this simplified situation. Unlike Babylon, Korea does not turn those 6 points into 9; its advantage here is specialist Science.

**Recheck:** If the ninth citizen could soon work a strong tile, one slot may be better initially. Revisit [assignments](/mechanics/resources-improvements/) and final Science after growth, a completed Farm or lost external food.

## Teaching situation two: the capital University is one turn away

**Conditions:** Suppose currently researchable technologies cost 100, 200, 300 and 500 when the capital University finishes next turn. This cost list is constructed to explain the formula.

**Choice:** The median is (200 + 300) ÷ 2 = 250, and the award is 250 × 0.5 = **125 Science**. Evaluate that alongside the building's ongoing value and finish on a useful schedule.

**Cost:** Delaying loses ongoing University returns and can postpone specialists or important technologies. A University in another city does not trigger this award; neither does a wonder without science relevance.

**Recheck:** Record researchable technologies and progress before completion, then inspect research afterward. Recalculate if the actual list differs. Failure to finish an entire technology does not mean the ability failed.
